# Level Touch / Test Log

Purpose: measure how often a level holds on each visit, without assuming the answer.
Levels hold twice often, but that can't be counted on, so the count is the evidence.

## Definitions

- **Touch** = only wicks reach the level on the judging frame; no body does.
- **Test** = a body on the judging frame closes at or through the level.
- **Judging frame:** 1h levels → 5m bodies. 4h levels → 15m bodies.
- **Held** = the hourly closes back on the level's side. **Lost** = the hourly closes through it.
- **Visit #** counts every return to the level (touches and tests), in order.
